25 Nutley Road is an impressive and substantial semi-detached family home extending to approximately 256 sq m (2,750 sq ft) over all three floors, with an even larger area again set in mature gardens to front and rear, ideally positioned on this highly regarded residential road in Ballsbridge, Dublin 4.

Situated between Nutley Lane and Dublin's embassy belt on Ailesbury Road, the property enjoys an enviable central location on Nutley Road within easy reach of the many amenities associated with Dublin 4.

These include a wide variety of prestigious schools, up to and including university in UCD, hospitals, both public and private, sports clubs catering for all tastes, parks, pubs and restaurants, sports stadia including the Aviva and RDS. travel options including the DART and a Quality Bus Corridor offering regular and speedy service, shops including the Merrion Centre and last, but not least, Sandymount beach.

Many of the above are within walking distance of 25 Nutley Road.

Occupying a generous site, the property combines extensive and versatile accommodation with a particularly attractive long, wide and peaceful rear garden extending to approximately 26 metres (85 feet) in length and 12m (39 feet) in width with mature planting, creating a private and secluded setting that will appeal strongly to families.

The garden is also accessible by a covered side entrance.

Internally, the accommodation is generously proportioned and offers excellent flexibility for modern family living.

At ground floor level there are two adjoining reception rooms, a spacious dining room, kitchen, study/storeroom and guest cloakroom.

The first floor provides four bedrooms, including a principal bedroom with en-suite shower room, together with a family bathroom and separate WC.

In addition, at the attic level, there is a newly refurbished suite to the highest standards incorporating a large bedroom and separate shower and bathroom providing further valuable accommodation.
